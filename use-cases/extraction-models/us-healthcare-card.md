# US Healthcare Card

## US Healthcare Card

Documentation for the data schema of the US Healthcare Card model template.

## Create Your US Healthcare Card Model

* Click on "Create your document AI model" in your dashboard, then select **"US Healthcare Card"**.
* The US Healthcare Card model template comes pre-configured with [standard fields](us-healthcare-card.md#us-healthcare-card-fields).
* Once your model is created, you can immediately [test](../../models/live-test.md) with your own documents.
* Optionally, you can adjust the model's [Data Schema](../../extraction-models/data-schema.md) if you need to modify fields.
