# Bank Check

Documentation for the data schema of the Bank Check model template.

## Create Your Bank Check Model

* Click on "Create your document AI model" in your dashboard, then select **"Bank Check".**
* The Bank Check model template comes pre-configured with [standard fields](bank-check.md#bank-check-fields).
* Once your model is created, you can immediately [test](../../models/live-test.md) with your own documents.
* Optionally, you can adjust the model's [Data Schema](../../extraction-models/data-schema.md) if you need to modify fields.
