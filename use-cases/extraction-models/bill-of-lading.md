# Bill of Lading

Documentation for the data schema of the Bill of Lading model template.

## Create Your Bill of Lading Model

* Click on "Create your document AI model" in your dashboard, then select **"Bill of Lading"**.
* The Bill of Lading model template comes pre-configured with [standard fields](bill-of-lading.md#bill-of-lading-fields).
* Once your model is created, you can immediately [test](../../models/live-test.md) with your own documents.
* Optionally, you can adjust the model's [Data Schema](../../extraction-models/data-schema.md) if you need to modify fields.
